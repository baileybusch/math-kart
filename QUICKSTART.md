# 🎮 QUICK START - Play Math Kart NOW!

## For iPad/Tablet (Easiest!)

**Just open this link in Safari:**

### 🌐 https://baileybusch.github.io/math-kart/

Works on older iPads too, including the **iPad mini 2 on iOS 12**. Turn the
iPad sideways (landscape) for the biggest track.

- Hold the big green **GO** button with one thumb and steer with the other
- Tap math answers with your finger
- No installation needed!

---

## For Parent: How to Run Locally (Alternative)

If you want to run it on your computer instead:

1. **Install & Run** (one command):
   ```bash
   npm install && npm run dev
   ```

2. **Open your browser** to the URL shown (usually `http://localhost:5173/math-kart/`)

3. **Start racing!** Use Arrow Keys or WASD to drive

---

## What Your Child Will Do

1. **Pick a track** on the menu (Forest to start, Desert unlocks in the Shop)
2. **Tap START RACE**, wait for 3-2-1-GO!
3. **Drive to the yellow stars.** The yellow arrow next to your kart points to the next one
4. **Solve the Math Stop** at each star - a right answer gives 5 coins! 💰
5. **Race 2 laps** (3 stars per lap) and cross the checkered line
6. **Win prize coins** (1st place = 50!) and visit the **Shop**

## Controls

### iPad/Tablet
- **Blue ◀ ▶ buttons** (bottom-left): steer
- **Green GO** (bottom-right): drive
- **Red BRAKE**: slow down / back up
- **II** (top-right): pause, keep racing, or quit to the menu
- Two fingers at once work (GO + steer)

### Desktop Computer
- **Arrow Keys** ⬆️⬇️⬅️➡️ or WASD to drive
- **Click numbers** for math answers

## Tips for Fun

- Stay on the road - grass slows you down!
- Try to get 1st place for maximum coins
- Unlock the Desert track (costs 100 coins)
- Buy Speed and Steering upgrades

The game teaches:

- 2-digit addition & subtraction

**Multiplication Facts:**
- Times tables (2, 5, 10)

**Division Facts:**

## Troubleshooting

**Getting the newest version on the iPad (Safari has no "hard refresh")**
1. Open the link with a new number on the end, e.g.
   `https://baileybusch.github.io/math-kart/?v=3`
   (any number you haven't used before forces a fresh download).
2. Still old or blank? **Settings → Safari → Advanced → Website Data →**
   search "github" → swipe left → **Delete**, then reopen the link.
   (This also resets coins.)
3. If you added Math Kart to the **Home Screen**, delete that icon and add it
   again after step 1.

**See a "Math Kart couldn't start" card?**
- Tap **Try Again**. If it keeps happening, do step 2 above.
- The small grey "Details for grown-ups" line says what went wrong - include
  it if you report a problem.

**Screen looks small on a phone?**
- Turn the phone sideways.

**Reset progress?**
- Settings → Safari → Advanced → Website Data → delete "github.io"
- On a computer: open the browser console and run
  `localStorage.removeItem('mathKartSave')`, then refresh
